## TL;DR

On a textbook **strong up-trend day** (NIFTY +1.6%, BANKNIFTY +1.72%, both closed at the day's high), the engine ran cleanly but **traded against the trend**: all **7 real trades were counter-trend shorts (BUY PUT), 14% win rate, −2.01R**, while the trend-following strategy that generated **+52R of with-trend BUY CALL signals was blocked by filters and took zero live trades**. This is a **filter-calibration failure**, not a bad-strategy-idea failure — the filters passed the losing strategies and blocked the winning ones.

## 6. Prioritized improvements

1. **Per-strategy RVOL / efficiency thresholds.** `LOW_RVOL` blocked +52R of EMA_Pullback profit. Lower/remove `rvol_threshold` for pullback/trend strategies; validate each filter per-strategy from the counterfactual history before applying it globally.
2. **Portfolio-level trend alignment.** On `day_type = "Trend Day ↑"` (trend-quality > 0.7), tilt toward trend-aligned strategies and throttle counter-trend shorts. The engine took 7 shorts / 0 longs on a day that closed at the highs.
3. **Cap repeats on the same level ("Woodchopper").** The report's own research queue flagged *BANKNIFTY BREAKOUT BUY PUT — 22 entries / 5 days, −57.4R on the same level.* Add `max_attempts_per_level = 2` to the thesis dedup.
4. **Demote the live losers, promote the shadow winner.** Move `ATR_Squeeze` / `Structural_v3.2` to shadow-only until expectancy turns positive; promote `EMA_Pullback` toward live.
5. **Fix option/expiry resolution.** Populate the option-snapshot warehouse (or fix the quotes call) and cache the resolved weekly expiry once per day, so combos can trade and directional pricing stops guessing.
6. **Fix the complacent auto-health verdict.** `strategy_health` reported *"✅ No strategy changes. System is performing as designed"* after a −2R real / −107R shadow day with the best strategy blocked. The health logic is blind to "best strategy filtered out" and "traded against a strong trend" — it must incorporate the blocked-winner / counterfactual signal.
7. **Start on time** — be up and pulsing by 09:15–09:20 to capture the open.

## 7. Important caveats

- **R magnitudes are inflated.** P&L is still computed in **index-point space, not option-premium space** (the known deferred issue). So a "+27R" win is *directionally* real but *numerically* overstated — trust the **ranking** of these findings, not the absolute R values.
- **The research engine itself is working as designed** — the counterfactual data cleanly proves which filters help vs hurt. The gap is that **nothing acts on that signal yet**: filters are static and global instead of per-strategy and expectancy-driven.
